# Initial Concept
Full-stack веб-додаток для управління комунальними послугами: лічильники, показники споживання, постачальники, тарифи та адреси. Побудований на Laravel 13 + Inertia.js v2 + React 19.

# Product Guide: Komunalka

## Vision
Komunalka is a self-hosted, full-stack utility management application designed for homeowners and property managers. It simplifies the complex task of tracking utility consumption, billing, and payments across multiple addresses, providing users with complete control over their data in a private, modern infrastructure.

## Target Audience
- **Homeowners:** Individuals tracking their own utility consumption and billing for one or more properties.
- **Property Managers:** Professionals overseeing multiple physical addresses and utility units.

## Core Objectives
1. **Simplify Billing:** Streamline the tracking and management of utility bills, payments, and service providers.
2. **Track Consumption:** Monitor and analyze consumption patterns across different utility services (water, gas, electricity, etc.).
3. **Manage Addresses:** Efficiently manage utility data and meters for multiple physical locations.

## Key Features (MVP & Beyond)
- **Address Management:** CRUD operations for physical addresses and service types.
- **Meter Tracking:** Management of utility meters, including consumption readings and photo documentation.
- **Provider & Tariff Management:** Tracking of service providers and their respective utility tariffs.
- **Billing & Payments:** Simplified tracking of upcoming bills and historical payment data.
- **Notifications (Future):** Automated reminders for upcoming bill deadlines and payment due dates.

## Unique Selling Point
Komunalka stands out as a **self-hosted, privacy-first solution**. By running the application on their own infrastructure (via Docker), users maintain absolute ownership and control over their sensitive utility and financial data.
